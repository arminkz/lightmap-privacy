Lightmap — Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

Lightmap does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Your best scores and your selected game mode are saved only on your device. They are never sent to the developer or anyone else. The app makes no network requests and contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking.

If you use the Share button, the text you share goes only where you choose to send it.

If you test a beta through TestFlight, Apple handles that program and may share information you submit (such as feedback, screenshots, crash reports, and your TestFlight email) with the developer, under Apple's TestFlight terms.
